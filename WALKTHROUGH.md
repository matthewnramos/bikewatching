# Lab 7 recording script

Record your laptop browser and voice, then export an MP4 under one minute. Open the published Bikewatching map. Keep the time slider, map, and legend visible. Choose a takeaway that matches what you learned.

**0–15 seconds: present the map.** Start at Any time and point to the green lanes and station circles.

“This is Bikewatching, a map of Bluebikes traffic in Boston and Cambridge during March 2024. Green lines show bike lanes. Larger circles show more station traffic, and the colors distinguish more departures, balanced traffic, and more arrivals.”

**15–40 seconds: demonstrate the interactions.** Pan, zoom in and out, hover over a station, move the slider to morning and evening, then choose Any time. Use Reset map if needed.

“I can pan and zoom while the station markers stay aligned. Hovering shows exact arrival and departure counts. The slider filters traffic within an hour of the selected time. Changing from morning to evening updates both the circle sizes and flow colors. Any time restores the full month.”

**40–55 seconds: explain a takeaway.** Leave the map visible.

“My main takeaway was how to keep filtering responsive with a large dataset. Grouping rides into minute buckets means each slider update examines only the relevant time window, including windows that cross midnight.”
